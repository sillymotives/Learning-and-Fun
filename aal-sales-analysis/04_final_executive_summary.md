# AAL Q4 2020 | Executive Business Summary

**Course submission companion.** The primary deliverable is the reproducible
JupyterLab notebook `notebooks/AAL_Sales_Analysis.ipynb`. This file is a
business-facing summary, not a substitute for the rubric-ordered notebook.

## Executive findings

- **Total recorded sales:** 340,302,500; **136,121 units** in **7,560**
  date-state-demographic-daypart records. The source does not identify currency.
- **Largest market by recorded sales:** Victoria 105,565,000.
  **Smallest market:** Western Australia 22,152,500.
- **Highest-selling group:** Men 85,750,000. **Lowest:** Seniors 84,037,500.
  All four groups contribute materially in every state; do not recommend
  eliminating a demographic category on this evidence.
- **Monthly recorded sales:** October 114,290,000; November 90,682,500;
  December 135,330,000. November is down 20.7% versus October on 30 recorded
  days per month. All seven states record less in November than October.
- **Time of day:** Morning 114,207,500; Evening 112,087,500. The national
  daypart difference is modest, and rows do not represent individuals.

## Recommended programme

**Pilot a targeted November sales promotion rather than discounting
nationwide.** Black Friday is a potential timing hook, but the dataset does
not establish an existing or successful promotion. Pilot against comparable
control stores/audiences, measure incremental **contribution profit after
costs**, and check whether sales were simply shifted from December.

Research targeted assortment/marketing trials for Women in Western
Australia and Seniors in Northern Territory while maintaining existing
ranges; the data does not show these groups prefer discounts.

A 15% discount plus 10% volume uplift yields 6.5% **less revenue** before
costs. Profitability cannot be established from the provided data.

## Key caveats

This is a course-provided teaching dataset. Every month has exactly 30
recorded days. The missing 31 October and 31 December do not establish
holiday closure. Transactions, store counts, margins, individual baskets,
prices, inventory, footfall, campaign histories, and other quarters are
not available. The ratio Sales = Units x 2,500 holds in every row.
